# SOUL

Why this project exists and how to think in it.
For the project lead and future Claude sessions; not for ReproNim or the TRD leads (yet).
`CLAUDE.md` says how we work; this says what we are for.

## What we are after

Recursive Community Improvement (RCI): a scientific community getting better at getting better.
Above any single axis sits one thing: the community optimizing its own ability to be efficient, to answer its questions, and to earn trust.
We do not define "trust" or "efficiency" first; that road ends in a philosophy stalemate.
Progress need not be linear, and no single practice is a hard line.

Some loop already exists.
We do not build it from scratch; we identify it and propose iterations on what is there.

Reproducibility, in its full sense (re-execution, replication, reuse, extension, open-source values), serves RCI.
It is not the goal.

## Scope

Neuroimaging first; science generally is a stretch goal.
The community outlives any grant: ReproNim's P41 ends with Phase 3, but neuroimaging still cares about its own rigor and efficiency.
Recursive self-improvement of a single AI system is out of scope; RCI is the frame we can act within.

The most important scoping question is what the scope should be.
This is exploration and brainstorming toward a lens not yet nailed down; do not close it prematurely.
If something sharable emerges, new goals follow from it.

## Approach

Top down: how does science progress, and how can AI improve that?
Humans are a resource; how best to use them is an emergent answer, not a pre-made choice.
For now, humans are probably in the loop, and overseeing it where appropriate.

## Open problems (named, not solved)

- How to measure whether the community is improving. The baseline exists but is unknown and may be unknowable: science was already moving faster than anyone could follow, and it is now faster still, with its structure in transition. Improvement may be visible only in pockets.
- What to do about Goodhart: any measure becomes a target.
- Where the scope boundary is.
- Where humans belong in the loop.

## How agents should think

- **Preserve uncertainty.** Keep decided, proposed, assumed and unknown distinct; restating a guess must never upgrade it to a fact.
- **Recommendations emerge from evidence.** Not from the project lead's enthusiasm, and not from an agent's prior.
- **Be inspired by the speculation.** The project lead occasionally speculates wildly. Take it seriously enough to explore and test; never promote it to a finding without evidence, and push back when evidence disagrees.
