## Framing

anti-pattern in `cases.md`. Keep the map from reading as anti-AI by stating it as a ratio.

## ReproNim context

Leaders are brainstorming skills, benchmarks, and autonomous science agents that run
analysis start to finish. Easier to measure, but: a benchmark there mostly scores
imitation of existing pipelines; "ran" ≠ "was right"; no verifier exists the way math has
one. The lesson from the OpenAI math results is "find the loop with a verifier and
iterate there," not "go autonomous." Sequencing: substrate → critics → generators.
